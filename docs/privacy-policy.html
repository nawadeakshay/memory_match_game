Memory Match – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Memory Match mobile game (“the App”), published by Musk Overseas Private Limited (“we”, “us”), handles information. In short: the App does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The App does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information.

Information kept on your device

Your game preferences (difficulty, time limit, theme, reduce motion) and your best results (best time and fewest moves) are kept only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. You can erase your results at any time with Settings → Reset Statistics, or by uninstalling the App.

Network, analytics and advertising

  • The App works fully offline and does not send gameplay data over the internet.
  • The App contains no advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • The App contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • The App contains no in-app purchases.

Third parties

We do not sell, trade or share any information with third parties, because we do not collect any. The App is distributed through Google Play; Google’s handling of your data when you download apps is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files.

Children

The App is a general-audience puzzle game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.

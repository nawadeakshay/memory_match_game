## Full description (4000 max)

```
Memory Match is a bright, friendly card-matching game that gives your brain a quick workout, anytime and anywhere.

Flip two cards. If the symbols match, they stay face up. If not, they flip back over, so remember where everything is! Clear the board in as few moves and as little time as you can.

★ HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a card to flip it, then tap a second card
• Matching symbols stay face up
• Find every pair to win

★ PICK YOUR CHALLENGE
• Easy – 6 pairs (4 × 3) for a quick warm-up
• Medium – 8 pairs (4 × 4) for a steady challenge
• Hard – 12 pairs (4 × 6) for true memory masters

★ BEAT THE CLOCK
Play relaxed with no timer, or set a time limit of 1, 1½, 2 or 3 minutes and race to clear the board before time runs out.

★ TRACK YOUR BEST
Your best time and fewest moves are shown on the home screen, so there's always a record to beat.

★ MADE TO FEEL GOOD
• Cheerful brain mascot and colorful card symbols
• Smooth card-flip animations
• Light, Dark or Auto theme
• Reduce Motion option for calmer play

★ PLAY YOUR WAY
• Works completely offline – no Wi-Fi needed
• No ads
• No account or sign-up
• No personal data collected

Great for a short brain break, a coffee-time puzzle, or keeping your mind sharp. A sharper mind is a happier you. Find every pair!
```
